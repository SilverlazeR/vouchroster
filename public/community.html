Our community

Relationships first. Always.

This isn’t a referral club with obligations, quotas, or forced introductions. It’s a place to get to know people worth knowing—and choose for yourself who you would recommend.

The way we see it

No pitches.
No pressure.
Just good people.

You can respect another local business owner without having to send them a customer. And when you do make a recommendation, it should mean something.

  • Connections you actually want to keep
  • Optional meetups instead of required attendance
  • No exclusive spots by industry
  • No scores, targets, recruitment quotas or commissions
A peek behind the scenes

A space for conversation, not competition.

A simple member area could help you find people in other industries, arrange a coffee, ask who knows a good specialist, and discover informal local get-togethers. Never a lead leaderboard.

Community principles

What we'll protect as we grow.

If this ever feels like a club where you owe somebody referrals, we've lost the plot.

Make it personal

Build relationships through real conversations, seeing each other's work, and helping when it makes sense.

Give without keeping score

No scorecards, no quotas, no points or pressure to reciprocate.

Trust is earned

Public recommendations need a genuine basis and a separate review.

Come as you are

Gatherings are optional and relaxed. Coffee, tours and conversations—not sales presentations.

The difference

You don't have to refer someone because they're here. They should be here because you'd refer them.

Members can be part of the community without automatically being listed as a publicly recommended professional.
