More than a directory

A community built on real relationships.

Not a room full of pitches. Not a weekly referral scoreboard. Just professionals who want to know good people, do great work, and look out for their neighbors.

  • Get to know people before you recommend them
  • Share introductions naturally, without expectation
  • Show up for optional get-togethers, or don't
  • Earn trust through your work—not membership

How good recommendations happen

Someone puts their name behind it.

A place in the community does not automatically make someone a recommended professional. Those are two different things.

See our standards →
01

Get to know them

A real relationship or genuine firsthand experience—not just a name on a list.

02

Make a nomination

Tell us who you trust, how you know them, and why you would recommend them.

03

Review with care

We review the recommendation and verify applicable credentials.

04

Keep earning trust

Recommendations stay accountable. We review concerns rather than ignoring them.

The promise

“Nobody pays to be on this list. They belong because someone believes in them.”

There are no fees, commissions, purchased rankings, mandatory referrals, or obligations to recommend another member. Just real people looking out for real people.
